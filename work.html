QD-CamNetz

The first 5G quantum campus network.

QD-CamNetz (Quanten-Drahtloses Campusnetzwerk) is an €8.5M research project led by TU Dresden together with TU München, IFW Dresden, CampusGenius and Deutsche Telekom, funded by the German Federal Ministry of Research. The goal is to integrate quantum technologies into working 5G infrastructure and build a campus network that can serve industrial applications.

My work sits between the quantum layer and the classical network. I architected the three-node hybrid quantum-classical framework built on deterministic semiconductor quantum-dot entanglement sources, including its functional interfaces, resource abstractions and control and data plane protocol interactions, and wrote the node-level processing and control software that distributes entanglement across the nodes.

The nodes are modular and relocatable, with integrated cryogenics, and are housed in standard telecom racks so they can be deployed in operational data centres. That moves entanglement distribution off the fixed optical bench, and fidelity held up under those conditions.

  • 95-98% State fidelity outside the optical bench
  • 0.45-0.48 Negativity (two-qubit maximum 0.5)
  • 7 ps Average sync precision, simulation
  • 4 Partner institutions coordinated

Timing is the hard requirement, because quantum links are only useful to higher-layer services when it is guaranteed. I developed entanglement-derived time synchronization that reaches 7 picoseconds average precision in simulation, along with shared randomness and access control mechanisms for synchronization-sensitive 5G/6G applications. Quantum randomness is treated as a managed resource across the 5G core, radio access and optical segments, which extends the reference architecture toward 6G.

On top of that plane I designed a quantum-secured architecture for privacy-preserving AI. Entanglement-derived keys from the quantum-dot sources protect federated machine learning traffic across a fibre mesh, and the design is benchmarked against state-of-the-art secure aggregation methods. In a full-stack simulation the pipeline withstands harvest-now-decrypt-later and eavesdropping attacks that break the classical cryptographic baseline. Cross-node orchestration software coordinates resource scheduling, streaming telemetry, verification workflows and system-level KPI evaluation.

The role goes beyond implementation. I turn stakeholder requirements into interface definitions and acceptance criteria, keep traceability from design assumptions to test evidence, and coordinate interfaces, scope and delivery milestones across physics, network engineering and outreach workstreams at four partner institutions. The work is documented in papers, tutorials, interface specifications, test procedures and runbooks so that a multi-institution consortium can repeat it and hand it over.

2026 · With Deutsche Telekom T-Labs

Patents

Three filings from the timing work.

Three applications submitted in partnership with Deutsche Telekom T-Labs. One is filed in the EU and two are in process. All three deal with giving mobile networks trustworthy picosecond timing without depending on satellites.

Quantum-Entangled Photon Timing for Coherent Joint Transmission

GNSS-independent picosecond synchronization of cooperating base stations for 5G-Advanced/6G coherent joint transmission and distributed MIMO, using entangled photons over fibre with statistical estimation and physical-layer integrity verification.

Oscillator Chain Extension for Distributed Timing Networks

Extends a quantum-verified timing reference to campus-scale 5G/6G networks over classical frequency-distribution chains, with trust-based control of downstream nodes and admission control for coherent transmission.

Scheduler-Coupled Spectral Entanglement Fabric for Quantum-Secured Synchronization in Distributed RANs

A scalable, scheduler-driven quantum timing fabric linking core nodes to lightweight spoke base stations for coherent joint transmission in distributed radio access networks, with tiered precision and integrity levels.

02 · Carrier programmes, Nokia

2018-2021 · Nokia ION / Centdoor · Nigeria

Nokia-Airtel IP-Microwave Pilot

Multi-site carrier deployment across radio and routing.

A carrier-scale deployment programme integrating IP/MPLS edge routing, Ethernet switching and microwave transport for Airtel's multi-site network. Over the course of the programme I brought 400+ edge routers, switches and microwave radios into service across the access layer, carrying QoS and VPN tunnelling over microwave-radio transport.

At that scale automation was necessary. I consolidated 250+ device integrations into a repeatable pipeline, and onsite and remote customer engineering fed field incidents back into the process.

  • 400+ Network elements commissioned
  • 250+ Automated device integrations

2018-2021 · Nokia ION / Centdoor · Nigeria

Service-Aware Aggregation Expansion

Backbone expansion on a live network.

Capacity expansion across the access and aggregation tiers of a network under continuous commercial load. Two backbone upgrades took the core from 10/40 to 100/400 Gbit/s across six sites and twelve routers; two WAN-SDN platform upgrades on Nokia NSP improved provisioning and telemetry along the way.

Most of the work was migration. I directed 25+ end-to-end traffic cutovers, each planned, risk-assessed and executed with near-zero service impact, and managed 20+ Service-Aware firmware rollouts with firewall policies, traffic filtering and access control configured to keep security posture and feature parity across the fleet.

  • 100/400G Backbone, up from 10/40G
  • 25+ Live traffic migrations
  • 20+ Firmware rollouts

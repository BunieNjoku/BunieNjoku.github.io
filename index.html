01 / Profile

About

I am a network and systems engineer. I design and run networks across the full stack: routing and transport at carrier scale, control and data plane protocol design, entanglement distribution, picosecond timing, orchestration and telemetry, and privacy-preserving machine learning on top.

In carrier deployment I commissioned over 400 IP/MPLS network elements, automated 250+ device integrations, and took backbones from 10/40 to 100/400 Gbit/s under live traffic. In quantum networking I build the control and data planes for a three-node testbed whose quantum-dot sources hold 95-98% entangled-state fidelity in standard telecom racks, with entanglement-derived synchronization at picosecond precision in simulation.
